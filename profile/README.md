## 🚀 What is AfroTender?

**AfroTender** is a comprehensive pan-African tender and procurement notification platform that aggregates government and private-sector tender opportunities from across the African continent. We empower businesses, consultants, and suppliers to discover, track, and respond to the right procurement opportunities at the right time.

Whether you are a small local business or a large multinational, AfroTender gives you the competitive edge you need to win contracts across Africa.

---

## 🎯 Key Features

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| 📢 **Real-Time Tender Alerts** | Receive instant email and in-app notifications for tenders matching your profile |
| 🔍 **Advanced Tender Search** | Filter by country, sector, budget, deadline, and more |
| 🌐 **Pan-African Coverage** | Tenders sourced from governments, NGOs, and private entities across African nations |
| 📋 **Tender Management** | Track applications, deadlines, and statuses from a single dashboard |
| 📊 **Market Intelligence** | Gain insights into procurement trends and awarded contracts |
| 🤝 **Supplier Directory** | Connect with verified suppliers, partners, and sub-contractors |
| 🔒 **Verified Listings** | All tenders are vetted and sourced from official and trusted publications |
